Mindset Scenes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Mindset Scenes uploader ("the app"), a private tool the owner of the Mindset Scenes YouTube channel uses to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • Read-only channel info (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel the owner signed into.

The app does not read, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and YouTube's own APIs. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token file from the owner's computer also ends the app's access.
