Mindset Scenes

Short animations about discipline and mindset.

Mindset Scenes is a YouTube channel of short, silent stick-figure animations about discipline, consistency, focus and the small daily choices that add up. Every video is one visual idea: two paths, one choice.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@mindsetscenes

About the Mindset Scenes uploader

The Mindset Scenes uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos to the Mindset Scenes YouTube channel, with their title, description and privacy setting. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other user's account or data.
